00 — Foundation

Why JOOD exists.

Cairo's rental market has a quiet problem. Properties sit underperforming — not because demand is weak, but because most landlords don't have the time, the tools, or the hospitality discipline to extract the full value of what they own. We close that gap.

The name JOOD comes from the Arabic جود — generosity, excellence in the act of giving. It defines how we treat guests and, by extension, how we structure partnerships with the people who trust us with their property.

We are not a listing service. We are a hospitality operations company that treats every unit as a guest-facing product — designed, photographed, priced dynamically, and managed with the same care you'd expect from a boutique hotel.

Our work is invisible to you, and absolute to your guest. When your unit performs, we perform. When it doesn't, you have a single number to call. His name is Hussam Hassan, and he answers on a Friday evening.

Every Airbnb booking is covered by AirCover — up to $3M USD in host damage protection. Beyond that, JOOD maintains a security-deposit system for every booking, conducts pre-arrival and post-checkout inspections, and documents unit condition photographically. Any damage is logged, reported to you the same day, and pursued through platform channels with our full support. Zero unresolved cases to date
Yes — for Models 01 and 02 the unit remains yours. You block dates in our shared calendar with at least two weeks' notice so we don't displace confirmed bookings. Model 03 is a full lease, so owner stays are not included — the trade-off being complete income predictability.
Model 01 — 60-day notice, no penalties, provided active bookings are honoured. Model 02 — 4-year minimum given the upfront design investment. Model 03 — fixed 5-year lease. All terms are written clearly in the contract, never buried in small print.
Under Model 01, the landlord owns all furniture — nothing changes at exit. Under Model 02, JOOD owns the FF&E we brought in. At exit you can buy it out at depreciated value, or we remove everything. Documented in the agreement from day one.
Routine operating costs — cleaning supplies, amenities, light bulbs — are covered by JOOD and deducted from gross revenue before the split. For any maintenance above 500 EGP, we contact you first with a description, photo and quote before proceeding. Major repairs remain landlord responsibility, but we coordinate everything via our trusted contractor network.
